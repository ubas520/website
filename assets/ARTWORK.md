## Final image generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: transparent website hero mascot cutout for Vetrix, a blue veterinary care brand. Create a beautiful photorealistic friendly golden retriever sitting upright, full body and paws completely in frame, one front paw raised in a gentle wave, happy open-mouth smile, bright kind brown eyes, exceptionally detailed soft golden fur, tasteful royal blue collar with a small silver paw-shaped tag. Soft premium studio lighting, warm approachable character, natural believable anatomy. Centered isolated subject on a truly transparent background, generous padding around ears and paws. No text, no logos, no frame, no scenery. This is a standalone dog asset to place beside a mobile app preview.
